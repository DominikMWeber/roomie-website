Roomie is a broker-less, room-to-room intercom built on the ESP32-S3. The goal is to introduce kids to real communication technology through actual conversations — with a setup that's fun to use and cuts down on screen time.

Upcoming posts will cover the technical build, setup process, and lessons learned from using it day to day.
